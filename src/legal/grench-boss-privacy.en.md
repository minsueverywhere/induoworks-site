# Grench Boss Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Grench Boss (including its demo, "the game") is developed by Frondward and published by Induo Works. This page explains what information the game handles.

## 1. Information the developer collects directly

The game has no developer servers or analytics tools. Nothing is sent to the developer automatically while you play.

## 2. Information recorded through Steam

- **Stats:** the game records progress steps (for example reaching a wave, rescuing the Kkomu, setting sail) and running totals (for example enemies defeated) through Steam stats. In Steamworks the developer sees only global totals (for example how many players reached wave 5). No information that identifies you is shared with the developer.
- **Achievements:** achievements earned in the full game are recorded on your Steam account.
- **Steam Cloud:** when enabled, your saves sync to Steam Cloud.
- **Steam game recording:** the game adds progress markers (for example a wave starting) to the Steam recording timeline. They stay in the recording on your PC and are not sent to the developer.

This information is processed by Valve's Steam service under the [Steam Privacy Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/).

## 3. Bug reports (only when you send one)

Only when you press "Send" in the in-game bug report window, the following goes to the developer's Discord channel:

- a screenshot of the game at the time of the report
- the recent game log (paths that contain your PC user name are masked)
- PC specs: operating system, CPU, memory size, graphics card, system language
- the game version and settings (language, resolution, graphics options and so on)
- your current progress and the campaign save file
- anything you write in the report

If you choose "Save" instead, the report stays on your PC and nothing is sent. Reports are used only to fix problems and are deleted once the problem is solved, or after one year at the latest.

## 4. Information stored on your PC

Saves, settings and logs are stored on your PC in `%APPDATA%\Frondward\GrenchBoss`. Versions up to 0.1.1 used `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\GrenchBoss`. You can delete this folder at any time.

## 5. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not sell your information or use it for advertising. Apart from Valve (Steam) and Discord (bug report delivery) as described above, nothing is shared.

## 6. Contact and deletion requests

To have a bug report you sent deleted, or for any other question, contact:

- Email: contact@induo.works

## 7. Changes

If this policy changes, the change will be announced on this page and in a Steam announcement.
